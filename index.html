OUR VISION

When a noob sees an Alpha, they should immediately get a good feeling.

The Alpha Clan exists to promote a friendly and non-toxic environment in GoBattle. We answer questions, we help new players and we treat everyone with respect.

To be part of it, you follow four simple rules. They should be pretty easy to follow 🙂

CLAN RULES

1

Beginner-friendly

If a noob asks a question, answer it. Try to avoid actively killing noobs in King Mode when possible.

2

Non-toxic, no scamming

Be respectful towards other players and have no history of scamming.

3

Clan name format

Change your name so we can all be recognised as one group:

The [Your Word] Alpha
4

Don't kill Alphas in King Mode

To avoid tension, we don't kill each other. You don't have to team up, but killing another Alpha is a no-go.

WARNINGBreaking these rules can result in an immediate removal from the clan.

  5. Change your name
