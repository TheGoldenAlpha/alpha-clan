QUESTIONS

How many members does the Alpha Clan have?

As of 3 October 2026: just over 40.

How do I join the Alpha Clan?

Either ask an Alpha Clan moderator in-game and go through the process together with them, or follow the Discord guide in the Join section on our homepage.

What are the benefits of being in the Alpha Clan?
  • Support from other Alpha members in King Mode
  • Lots of new friends who treat you kindly, in a non-toxic environment
  • Help when you need support with a ticket (for example if your English is not good enough or you run out of arguments)
  • Access to exclusive giveaways
  • Access to exclusive events, often with prizes

We are also open to new ideas and concepts! Every member is seen, if they want to be.

Who is the owner of the Alpha Clan?

We don't really have an owner. We see the Alpha Clan much more as a brotherhood. The founder comes closest: he founded the clan, made the first recruitments and sets the basic rules. Apart from that, he acts like a normal member.

How do I become a moderator in the Alpha Clan?
  • Very mature and professional behaviour, including pointing out mistakes to members
  • Being active on Discord
  • Representing the Alpha Clan well to the outside

A few recruitments can help, but they are not required.

How do I join the Discord?

Click the Discord button at the top in the header. If needed, create a Discord account (only an email is required). Then click Join and you're in!
